A paper on quasinormal-mode theory for time-varying media published in *Physical Review B*. The work develops a QNM framework for Floquet media slabs (structures whose material properties are modulated periodically in time), enabling modal analysis of parametric amplifiers, time-varying antennas, and non-reciprocal devices.
