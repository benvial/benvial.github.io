## Topic

Quasinormal modes (QNMs) are the natural resonances of open systems. Energy leaks to the surroundings, so their frequencies are complex: the real part sets the resonance and the imaginary part its decay rate. QNM expansions are well established in electromagnetism, and the talk extends them to flexural waves in thin elastic plates. Expanding the scattered field on a few modes gives a reduced order model that explains which resonances respond to a given excitation, and it costs far less than a direct solve at every frequency.
