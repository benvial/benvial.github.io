A worked example of a frequency-domain scattering simulation with the [gyptis](https://gyptis.gitlab.io) finite element package. It goes from Maxwell's equations to the scattering, absorption and extinction cross sections of a silicon rod, introducing perfectly matched layers, meshing and the weak form along the way, and checks every number against the exact Lorenz–Mie solution.

Press **Run live** to edit and re-run any cell in your browser. The code runs on a real gyptis kernel, started on mybinder.org from the environment in [benvial/gyptis-binder](https://github.com/benvial/gyptis-binder).
